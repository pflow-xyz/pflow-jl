🦠 SIR Epidemic Model - ODE Solver Demo

About: This demonstrates the psolver.js module simulating an SIR (Susceptible-Infected-Recovered) epidemic model using Petri nets. The model shows how a disease spreads through a population.

Parameters

Model Description

The SIR model divides the population into three compartments:

  • S (Susceptible): People who can catch the disease
  • I (Infected): People who have the disease and can spread it
  • R (Recovered): People who recovered and gained immunity

Transitions:

  • Infection: S + I → I + I (rate: β × S × I)
  • Recovery: I → R (rate: γ × I)

# Settle closure: a checkable predicate for the generated sub-SMC

Roadmap item P3 (`book-pflow-xyz/PROOF-ROADMAP.md`). Claim under test, from
"The Category Settle" and Appendix E ("Net Types as Sub-SMCs"):

> Settle is the sub-SMC of **OPetri** generated by two transition shapes,
> `send : balance -> pending` and `settle : pending -> balance`.

Code: `src/settle.jl`. Tests: `test/test_closure.jl`
(`julia --project=. -e 'using pflow, AlgebraicPetri; include("test/test_closure.jl")'`).

## What "closure" means here

**OPetri** is the category of open Petri nets as structured cospans
(AlgebraicPetri `Open`): a net with chosen boundary *species*, composed by
pushout along shared boundary species (`glue` in `src/algebraic.jl`) and
tensored by disjoint union (`merge`/`+` in `src/pflow.jl`). "Generated by
send and settle" means: the smallest class of open nets containing the two
generators that is closed under

1. **glue along balances** — pushout identifying boundary places named the
   same in different parts (`glue(parts, ports)`), and sequential composition
   of cospans (`compose(send_net(x,p), settle_net(p,y))`);
2. **⊗** — disjoint union followed by `to_labelled_petri_net`.

The *closed* members (no boundary) are the settlement networks: any number of
parties, any number of channels between them.

## What the predicate checks

`is_settle_generated(lpn)` (with `settle_reason(lpn)` giving a reason string
on rejection, and `settle_typing(lpn)` giving the inferred typing) decides
membership by the following characterisation: `lpn` is in the class iff there
is a typing `kind : S -> {balance, pending}` such that

- **(T1)** every transition has exactly one input and one output species,
  multiplicity 1, distinct (`ρ = 1`, no self-loops);
- **(T2)** every transition goes `balance -> pending` (then it is a `send`)
  or `pending -> balance` (a `settle`);
- **(T3)** every `pending` has exactly one producer and one consumer.

Balance places are unconstrained (any number of sends out and settles in,
including none). (T2) is a bipartiteness test on the place graph; (T3)
selects the pending side per connected component. When both sides qualify
(a 2-cycle `x -> t1 -> y -> t2 -> x`) both typings are valid and the net is
the channel `x -> x` with `y` as pending; the predicate prefers the side
whose labels start with `pending` for reporting, which does not change the
verdict.

For an `OpenLabelledPetriNet` the legs are **boundary** and a boundary
pending is exempt from (T3): it is half a channel. That is what makes the
generators themselves members (`send_net`, `settle_net`) while their closed
apexes are rejected with `pending-degree`.

Reason tags: `fan-in`, `fan-out`, `no-input`, `no-output`, `self-loop`,
`not-bipartite`, `pending-degree`.

## Catlab formulation

`SchSettleNet <: SchLabelledPetriNet` adds `Kind::AttrType`,
`skind : Attr(S, Kind)`, `tkind : Attr(T, Kind)`; `SettleNet =
SettleNetUntyped{Symbol,Symbol}`. `to_settle_net(lpn)` runs the inference
and records the typing (so a `SettleNet` value is a certificate that the
predicate passed); `is_well_typed_settle(sn)` checks a *recorded* typing
instead of inferring one; `forget_kinds(sn)` is the forgetful functor back
to `LabelledPetriNet`, computed as a Δ-migration along the schema
inclusion (`migrate(LabelledPetriNet, sn, FOb, FHom)`). Round trip
`forget_kinds(to_settle_net(lpn)) == lpn` is tested.

Not done: `OpenACSetTypes(SettleNetUntyped, :S)` to make `SettleNet` itself
an object of a structured-cospan category, so that composition would carry
the typing along and well-typedness of composites could be checked on
`SettleNet` directly rather than re-inferred after `glue`. Open-net
composition in this repo is over `LabelledPetriNet`; the typing is
re-inferred on the apex.

## What this does NOT establish

- **A checked predicate over random samples is not a proof of closure.**
  `test_closure.jl` draws 60 random wirings (2–6 parties, 1–7 channels,
  repeated channels allowed), 30 random ⊗ pairs and 20 ⊗-then-glue
  composites, and asserts every one is accepted. That is evidence that
  `glue`/`⊗` of members stay members on those samples; it is not a proof for
  all wirings. The proof is short on paper — gluing along *balance* places
  only changes balance degrees, which (T1)–(T3) never constrain, and ⊗
  changes nothing locally — but it is not machine-checked here. P4 (Lean)
  is where such statements are meant to be proved.
- **The predicate decides membership in the class described by (T1)–(T3).**
  That (T1)–(T3) coincides with "generated by send and settle" is argued,
  not proved: soundness (every composite satisfies T1–T3) is what the random
  check samples; completeness (every T1–T3 net is a composite) follows by
  reading each pending with its send and settle as one channel and gluing
  all channels along their balances — unproved here.
- **Nothing about behaviour.** Membership is structural (incidence
  matrix). Liveness, throughput (λ) and invariants across gluing are P2/P4.
- **Contextual arcs** (read/inhibitor) are not in OPetri at all;
  `to_labelled_petri_net` refuses them before the predicate runs.

## Tests (`test/test_closure.jl`, testset "Settle closure")

| testset | what it checks |
|---|---|
| `generators and channel` | `send_net`/`settle_net` are members as open nets, rejected when closed; `compose` of the two equals `settle_channel` (incidence matrix); empty net and lone balance are members; inferred typing matches the labels |
| `closed under glue along balances (random wirings)` | 60 random wirings accepted; 3-party cycle and 2-cycle accepted |
| `closed under ⊗ (disjoint union)` | 30 random ⊗ pairs accepted; 20 ⊗-then-glue composites accepted |
| `negative controls` | netting transition (ρ = 2) → `fan-in` naming `net_ab`, and `to_settle_net` throws; two-input `pay_fee` → `fan-in`; extra output → `fan-out`; weight-2 arc → `fan-in`; two sends into one pending → `pending-degree`; odd 3-cycle → `not-bipartite`; self-loop/source/sink → `self-loop`/`no-input`/`no-output` |
| `Catlab schema: SettleNet and the forgetful functor` | `to_settle_net` well-typed, `forget_kinds` round-trips on the channel and 10 random wirings; a hand-corrupted `skind` is caught |
